Import a recipe video

Paste a public Facebook Reel or Instagram post/reel. The server downloads a temporary copy, transcribes its audio with local Whisper, reads on-screen text with local OCR, and sends the combined evidence through a recipe-only Vercel AI Gateway cleaner before preparing a draft.

Supported public links include Facebook Reels and Instagram /p/, /reel/, and /tv/ posts. Private, login-only, or region-blocked posts may need a public link or a local downloader cookie file.

Evidence sources

Unticked sources are not downloaded, transcribed, or sent to the cleaner. At least one source must stay ticked.

What gets combined:

  • The post caption or description
  • Spoken instructions and quantities from local Whisper transcription
  • Ingredient cards and other on-screen text from local Tesseract OCR
  • A recipe-only AI Gateway pass that removes social chatter before final generation

Only the ticked sources above are combined. The video is temporary and deleted after extraction. The raw channels go only to the cleaner; you review the complete recipe before saving it.
