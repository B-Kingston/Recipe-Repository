Import a recipe video

Paste a public Facebook Reel or Instagram post/reel. The server downloads a temporary copy, transcribes its audio with local Whisper, reads on-screen text with local OCR, and sends the combined evidence through a recipe-only OpenRouter cleaner before preparing a draft.

Supported public links include Facebook Reels and Instagram /p/, /reel/, and /tv/ posts. Private, login-only, or region-blocked posts may need a public link or a local downloader cookie file.

What gets combined:

  • The post caption or description
  • Spoken instructions and quantities from local Whisper transcription
  • Ingredient cards and other on-screen text from local Tesseract OCR
  • A recipe-only OpenRouter pass that removes social chatter before final generation

The video is temporary and deleted after extraction. The raw channels go only to the cleaner; you review the complete recipe before saving it.
